TOWNKIT / FIELD GUIDE

Make a mess. Undo it.

A systems-first, single-file archipelago editor: a 2,048 × 2,048 meter world with three islands, eight destinations, editable neighborhoods and undeveloped terrain. Draw plots on empty land, sculpt the terrain, lay roads, then build and reshape connected rooms. Rooms and openings are deliberately forgiving.

Getting around: click a district in the overview, click the minimap to travel, or double-click terrain to zoom in. World or Home frames the archipelago. Café jumps into a six-room commercial layout. Blueprints (B) opens the library. Place on map starts stamping; R rotates and M mirrors. Plan frames the selected building from above. Roofs auto reveals interiors at building scale. You can build on empty ground, even off-road.

Try this: select the café, pull its shared wall into the service area, then shrink the window wall. Openings slide when they fit and become amber ghosts when they don't. Grow the wall or undo to recover them.

This slice: one building level, convex street-facing plots, road centerlines baked into editable segments, automatic water crossings, convex room pieces, and 8 m terrain samples. The terrain mesh refines to 2 m nearby for road/plot grading. Distant massing becomes detailed rooms at lot scale. Room overlap is carved from neighboring rooms. Doors show room-graph connectivity, not full NPC navigation. Twelve editable blueprint families, generated roof massing, street-facing polygon parcels, connected curved streets, and a saveable blueprint library. No furniture, WFC, traffic, or life simulation yet. The island is editable, not an inhabited simulation. New seed regenerates the whole world as one undoable action. Terrain and grading use spatially indexed 128 m rendering chunks.

Offline & local: all code is inside this file. No server, network requests, or telemetry. Autosave depends on your browser's local-file storage support; Save world is your portable JSON backup. Renderer: compact embedded WebGL, not Three.js. Desktop mouse + keyboard recommended.
