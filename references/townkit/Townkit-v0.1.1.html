TOWNKIT / FIELD GUIDE

Make a mess. Undo it.

A systems-first, single-file town-building prototype. Draw plots on empty land, sculpt the terrain, lay roads, then build and reshape connected rooms. Rooms and openings are deliberately forgiving.

Try this: select the café, pull its shared wall into the kitchen, then shrink the window wall. Openings slide when they fit and become amber ghosts when they don't. Grow the wall or undo to recover them.

This slice: one building level, rectangular plots, straight road segments, convex room pieces, simple heightfield terrain. Room overlap is carved from neighboring rooms. Doors show room-graph connectivity, not full NPC navigation. No furniture, WFC, roofs, traffic, or simulation yet.

Offline & local: all code is inside this file. No server, network requests, or telemetry. Autosave depends on your browser's local-file storage support; Save world is your portable JSON backup. Renderer: compact embedded WebGL, not Three.js. Desktop mouse + keyboard recommended.
